## Privacy and sharing boundary

Participant-level processed proteomics and clinical metadata should not be redistributed publicly unless explicit permission or public-data terms allow it. Public outputs should be limited to scripts, aggregate summaries, non-sensitive figures, module definitions and manuscript text.
