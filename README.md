# TB Undernutrition Serum Proteomics

Computational companion repository for the study:

**Serum nutritional-metabolic recovery during tuberculosis treatment across BMI-defined undernutrition strata: A longitudinal proteomic reanalysis**

## Scientific scope

This project examines longitudinal serum-proteomic recovery during pulmonary tuberculosis treatment across baseline BMI-defined undernutrition strata.

The central publication-facing construct is the **serum nutritional-metabolic recovery axis**, derived from coordinated lipid/cholesterol/apolipoprotein transport, nutritional-carrier biology, and endocrine/systemic immunometabolic signalling.

The analyses support recovery of this molecular axis during treatment in both baseline BMI strata. Baseline BMI does not define the molecular axis and does not significantly modify its longitudinal trajectory.

## Source study and data provenance

This is a secondary reanalysis of processed longitudinal serum-proteomics abundance data and linked clinical and TMT sample-mapping files generated by the Jacobs/Powell team.

The corresponding source-study mass-spectrometry data are deposited under:

- ProteomeXchange/PRIDE: **PXD040546**
- MassIVE: **MSV000091392**

The collaborator-supplied processed abundance matrix, clinical metadata, TMT sample-linkage files, and participant-level reconstructed analytical datasets are **not redistributed in this repository**.

See `DATA_ACCESS.md`.

## Repository contents

- `results/aggregate_module_results/` — sanitized aggregate module-level statistical outputs.
- `results/recovery_axis/` — publication-facing recovery-axis supplementary results.
- `figures/` — locked publication figure in multiple formats.
- `data_templates/` — generic input-format templates only; no participant data.
- `scripts/` - portable publication-facing four-stage analysis workflow.
- `resources/` - participant-independent curated module-to-protein mapping used by the public workflow.
- `docs/` — methodological and reproducibility documentation.
- `provenance/` — release provenance and cryptographic checksums.

## Important interpretation boundary

The serum nutritional-metabolic recovery axis is not a vitamin measure, malnutrition score, clinical nutritional index, or direct intracellular functional measurement.

It represents coordinated longitudinal variation in circulating protein systems related to lipid transport, nutritional-carrier biology, and endocrine-immunometabolic signalling.
